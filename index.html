Sam Dang

I am an M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at UC San Diego, working in the Existential Robotics Lab with Prof. Nikolay Atanasov. My research is on safe learning-based control for robots: combining learned world models with safety frameworks such as Hamilton-Jacobi reachability and control barrier functions.

Previously, I worked on perception and trajectory prediction for ground robots with Prof. Sonia Martínez at UCSD, and received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Wyoming, where I worked with Prof. Suresh Muknahallipatna on generative models for a snowplow collision project.

Object-Centric Latent Safety Filter with Pairwise Failure Attribution
Sam Dang, Nikolay Atanasov
Submitted to Learning for Dynamics and Control (L4DC), 2027
Paper (coming soon)

A latent safety filter on an object-centric world model. A pairwise safety margin scores every pair of objects, and attributes each intervention to the pair that causes it.

Projects
Control Barrier Function & Hamilton-Jacobi Reachability Safety Filters
Personal project

Extending an open-source CBF-QP safety filter to a double integrator with high-order CBFs and an HJ reachability filter, and comparing their safe sets and intervention timing.

Neural Control Barrier Function Synthesis via Attention
Course project, UC San Diego, 2025

Compared MLP and multi-head attention architectures for learning policy neural CBFs on a Segway. Attention improved safe-set accuracy but yielded more CBF-QP safety violations.
